Solemnity Feast of Sacred Heart of Jesus  Gospel Msg ( Jesus Thanks His Father) Jesus said, " Come to me, all you that are weary and are carrying heavy burden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me; for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light." MT(11:28-30) Jesus is Lord, spread the living word of God.
