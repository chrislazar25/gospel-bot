Solemnity Feast of St.Joseph ( Dream of Joseph) Angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream and said, " Joseph son of David, do not be afraid to take Mary as your wife, for the child conceived in her is from the Holy Spirit. She will bear a son, and you are to name him Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins." MT(1:20-21) Lord, increase our faith like St.Joseph.
