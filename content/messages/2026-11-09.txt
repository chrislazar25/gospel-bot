Dedication of the Lateran Basilica Gospel Msg ( Jesus Cleanses the Temple) His disciples remembered the words of scripture, "Zeal for your house will consume me." At this the Jews answered and said to him, "What sign can you show us for doing this?" Jesus answered and said to them, "Destroy this temple and in three days I will raise it up."
