Feast of Annunciation ( Immaculate Conception) Then the angel said to her, "Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God. Behold, you will conceive in your womb and bear a son, and you shall name him 'Jesus'. Mary said, " Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord. May it be done to me according to your word." Then the angel departed from her. LK(1:26-38) Jesus is Lord, spread the living word of God.
