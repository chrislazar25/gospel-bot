Today's Gospel Msg ( Jesus Cures Many People ) Great crowds came to him, bringing with them the lame, the maimed, the blind, the mute, and many others. They put them at his feet, and he cured them, so that the crowd was amazed when they saw the mute speaking, the maimed whole, the lame walking, and the blind seeing. And they praised the God of Israel. MT(15:30-31) Jesus is Lord, spread the living word of God.
