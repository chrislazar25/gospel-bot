## What happens while a date is unresolved

An unsupported reference, missing message, or held quotation does not trigger a random replacement. These dates remain unresolved in the generated packet. The proposals above require human review and transfer into the approved content workflow before use.

The production release requires fourteen consecutive approved dates. An incomplete batch blocks that release. At runtime, missing approved content blocks sending. The bot never generates a fallback message. Nothing in this document enables sends or publishes content to Neon.
